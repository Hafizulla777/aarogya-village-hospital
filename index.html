Honest Care

We treat what a village actually needs

We are a small OPD hospital — not a surgical centre. If a case needs surgery or advanced care, we honestly refer you to the district hospital instead of doing unnecessary procedures here.

  • Fever, infections and seasonal diseases — tested & treated in-house
  • BP, sugar and thyroid long-term follow-up with monthly medicines
  • Child vaccination as per the government immunisation chart
  • Pregnancy ANC checkups with mother-and-baby monitoring
  • Minor dressing, stitches for small wounds, tetanus injection
  • Honest referral for surgical or serious cases — no fake treatment
