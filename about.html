Our Story

Founded to Address Rural Healthcare Gaps

Residents of the village faced significant challenges accessing timely medical care. A simple fever required a two-hour journey to the nearest town hospital, leading many families to delay essential medical treatment or seek medications from unlicensed sources. This situation often resulted in minor health issues escalating into more serious conditions.

Aarogya Village Hospital was established in 2014 as a modest one-room Outpatient Department (OPD). From these humble beginnings, we have grown into a clean, two-story facility equipped with an in-house laboratory and pharmacy, while maintaining our commitment to personalized care where every patient is recognized by name.

Our Core Philosophy: We provide treatment for conditions within our scope of practice, conduct necessary diagnostic tests before prescribing medications, and refer patients honestly to appropriate higher-level facilities when specialized care is required.
